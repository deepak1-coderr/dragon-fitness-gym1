Floor Etiquette

RULES & DISCIPLINE

A clean, respectful floor is what makes serious training possible. Six rules, no exceptions.

01

Proper Attire

Gym shoes and appropriate workout attire required at all times.

02

Wipe Equipment

Clean machines, benches, and mats after every use.

03

Re-Rack Weights

Return every plate, dumbbell, and bar to its place.

04

Respect the Floor

No shouting, no dropping weights carelessly, no distractions.

05

Punctuality

Arrive on time for booked sessions and classes.

06

Locker Etiquette

Secure your belongings — the gym isn't responsible for items left out.
